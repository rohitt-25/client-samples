About us

Care for your teeth

Every day involves checking for cavities, filling teeth, and helping people manage tooth pain. We take time to examine what is going on and explain the treatment before anything starts.

You will always be treated gently, with clean instruments and a quiet room to help you relax.

How we can help

Treatments

Here are the everyday treatments we offer to keep your mouth healthy.

01

Routine Cleanings

Removing plaque and tartar to keep your gums healthy.

02

Tooth Fillings

Repairing cavities with tooth-coloured materials.

03

Root Canal Treatment

Cleaning infected tissue inside the tooth to stop pain.

04

Dental Crowns

Covering weakened teeth to restore bite and strength.

05

Tooth Extractions

Safely removing damaged or crowded teeth when necessary.

06

Dental Implants

Replacing missing teeth with sturdy artificial roots.
